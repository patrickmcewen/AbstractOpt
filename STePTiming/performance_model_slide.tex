\begin{center}
\Large\textbf{STeP Analytical Performance Model}
\end{center}

\medskip

\small
Each node $n$ in the DAG is characterized by four quantities derived from its operator type and stream shapes:

\smallskip

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\begin{tabular}{@{} l l l @{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
$T_{\text{fire}}(n)$ & Firing time & Processing time per input step (roofline) \\
$N_{\text{fire}}(n)$ & Firing count & Total number of firings \\
$\mathit{NIT}^{n'}_n$ & Input tile count & Input tiles consumed from predecessor $n'$ per output chunk \\
$\mathit{OTPC}(n)$ & Output tiles per chunk & Output tiles produced per output chunk \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
\normalsize

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{2.2}
\begin{tabular}{@{} >{\raggedright}p{3.2cm} p{12.5cm} @{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Quantity} & \textbf{Equation} \\
\midrule
Firing Time \newline\small(roofline per step)
    & $T_{\text{fire}}(n) = \max\!\left(T_{\text{PMU-read}},\; \left\lceil\frac{\mathit{FLOPs}}{\mathit{BW}_c}\right\rceil,\; T_{\text{PMU-store}}\right)$ \\
Start Time \newline\small(predecessor gating)
    & $\mathit{st}(n) = \displaystyle\max_{n' \in \text{pred}(n)} \bigl(\mathit{fto}(n')\bigr)$ \\
First Tile Out \newline\small(latency to first output)
    & $\mathit{fto}(n) = \mathit{st}(n) + \underbrace{(\mathit{NIT} - 1) \cdot \max(T_{\text{fire}},\, \mathit{OTI}(n'))}_{\text{input chunk delay}} + T_{\text{fire}}(n)$ \\
Output Chunk Interval \newline\small(steady-state rate)
    & $\mathit{OCI}(n) = \displaystyle\max_{n' \in \text{pred}(n)} \Bigl(\mathit{NIT}^{n'}_n \cdot \max\bigl(T_{\text{fire}}(n),\, {\mathit{OTI}}(n')\bigr)\Bigr)$ \\
Output Tile Interval
    & $\mathit{OTI}(n) = \dfrac{\mathit{OCI}(n)}{\mathit{OTPC}(n)}$ \\
End Time \newline\small(node completion)
    & $\textit{end}(n) = \mathit{fto}(n) + \left(N_{\text{fire}}(n) - 1\right) \cdot \mathit{OTI}(n)$ \\
Graph Time \newline\small(program total)
    & $T_{\text{graph}} = \displaystyle\max_{n \in \text{leaf}(G)} \bigl(\textit{end}(n)\bigr)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip

\small
\textbf{Also modeled:}
\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
\item \textbf{HBM contention} --- two-pass estimation: solve without contention to find overlapping off-chip ops, then recompute with rate-weighted concurrent requests across shared channels.
\item \textbf{One-shot predecessor correction} --- when a predecessor produces only one output chunk ($N_{\text{fire}}(n') \leq \mathit{NIT}$), its OTI is replaced by $T_{\text{fire}}$ to avoid propagating a decoupled rate.
\item \textbf{Burst output (OTPC $> 1$)} --- expansion and triggered-load operators produce multiple tiles per chunk; OTI distributes the chunk interval across all output tiles.
\item \textbf{Fan-out / fan-in routing} --- FlatPartition multiplies effective OTI by consumer count $N_c$; FlatReassemble divides by data input count $N_d$.
\item \textbf{Dynamic dimensions} --- symbolic variables resolved via expected-value substitution assuming uniform routing, or by concrete trace instantiation.
\end{itemize}
